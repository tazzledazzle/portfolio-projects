# LEAD-E-010 — Make sound architectural trade-offs

## Best practices

- Frame one decision with its constraints, stakeholders, and success criteria before comparing solutions.
- Document at least two viable alternatives using the same decision criteria.
- State both the benefit selected and the downside deliberately accepted.
- Record uncertainty, dissent, and a trigger for revisiting the choice.
- Publish the decision as an ADR that preserves reasoning, not merely the selected technology.
- Compare predicted consequences with observed outcomes after the decision has operated.

## Four habits

1. Write Alternative A and Alternative B before recommending a choice.
2. Use the same weighted or qualitative criteria for every alternative.
3. State the accepted trade-off and unresolved uncertainty in the decision record.
4. Schedule a consequence review with evidence and a reversal trigger.
