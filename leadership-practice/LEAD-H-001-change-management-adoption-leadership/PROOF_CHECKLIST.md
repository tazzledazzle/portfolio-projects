# Proof checklist — LEAD-H-001

- [ ] Attach a rollout plan naming consumer segments, champions, owners, and the established change being adopted.
- [ ] Show at least two migration waves with readiness, entry, exit, pause, and rollback criteria.
- [ ] Provide an adoption curve with numerator, denominator, data source, cadence, and known limitations.
- [ ] Include qualitative friction evidence from office hours, support, surveys, or champion feedback.
- [ ] Show a dated change to enablement, support, compatibility, or sequencing based on that feedback.
- [ ] Record a wave-level safety or operating signal, not adoption percentage alone.
- [ ] Demonstrate final ownership, support transition, and any remaining consumers or exceptions.
- [ ] Confirm all people and metrics are truthful, privacy-safe, and redacted where necessary.
