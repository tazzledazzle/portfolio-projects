# LEAD-H-001 — Change management / adoption leadership

## Best practices
- Segment consumers by readiness, risk, dependency, and migration complexity before assigning rollout waves.
- Recruit champions with explicit responsibilities for feedback, local enablement, and escalation rather than using names as symbolic endorsements.
- Define entry and exit criteria for each wave, including rollback or pause triggers when user or operational harm appears.
- Reduce migration friction with compatibility windows, documentation, examples, office hours, and a clearly owned support path.
- Track an adoption curve with numerator, denominator, source, cadence, and known limitations instead of reporting a context-free percentage.
- Combine quantitative adoption with qualitative friction and support signals to decide what to improve before the next wave.
- Separate rollout execution from pattern definition and ROI funding: this kit proves people adopted an established direction safely.

## Four habits
1. Confirm champion commitments and consumer readiness before opening a rollout wave.
2. Publish wave entry, exit, pause, and rollback criteria with named owners.
3. Review adoption and friction signals on a fixed cadence with source and denominator visible.
4. Close each feedback loop by updating enablement, support, or sequencing before expanding.
