# LEAD-I-001 — Stakeholder management across consumers

## Best practices

- Write a shared platform problem and success measure before comparing requests from consuming teams.
- Publish scoring criteria, weights, evidence expectations, decision cadence, and the accountable decision owner.
- Normalize requests into comparable units while retaining each consumer’s constraints, urgency, and affected scope.
- Separate evidence from advocacy and record uncertainty rather than rewarding the loudest stakeholder.
- Share scores, ties, trade-offs, and final rationale; provide an appeal or evidence-update path.
- Revisit priorities when inputs change, while keeping adoption-wave planning and influence currencies outside this artifact.

## Four habits

1. Confirm the shared problem and success measure with consumers before intake scoring.
2. Require the same evidence fields from every consuming team.
3. Publish scores and rationale on a predictable decision cadence.
4. Close every decision with owner, next checkpoint, and conditions that would change priority.
