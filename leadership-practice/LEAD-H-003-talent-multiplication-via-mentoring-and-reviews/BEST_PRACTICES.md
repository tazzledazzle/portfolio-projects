# LEAD-H-003 — Talent multiplication via mentoring and reviews

## Best practices

- Define multiplication as more engineers independently producing strong outcomes, not the leader completing more work.
- Track a privacy-safe portfolio across multiple engineers, roles, and opportunities rather than presenting one mentee's plan as organizational evidence.
- Delegate consequential design, review, and stakeholder responsibilities with clear guardrails and visible sponsorship.
- Turn repeated review lessons into shared examples, rubrics, or patterns that improve the team's median.
- Inspect the distribution of ownership and review participation so opportunities do not repeatedly flow to the same people.
- Measure durable outcomes such as independent leads, broader reviewer coverage, reduced leader bottlenecks, and successors for critical areas.
- Record counter-evidence and attribution limits; team growth has multiple causes and should not be claimed solely by the leader.

## Four habits

1. Review the distribution of consequential opportunities across the team.
2. Sponsor one qualified engineer into a visible stretch role with explicit support.
3. Convert one repeated review lesson into a reusable team learning surface.
4. Remove yourself from one decision path after another engineer demonstrates readiness.
