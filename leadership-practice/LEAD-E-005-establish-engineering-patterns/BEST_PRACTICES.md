# LEAD-E-005 — Establish engineering patterns

## Best practices

- Extract a pattern from repeated, demonstrated needs rather than declaring a standard after one example.
- Record the context, forces, recommended approach, exceptions, and a worked example so teams can apply the pattern without oral history.
- Publish an intentionally small catalog that makes the preferred path easier than bespoke configuration.
- Track adoption by consuming teams and successful uses, not document views.
- Name a maintainer and review date for every entry so obsolete guidance is revised or retired.
- Separate pattern ownership from rollout-wave planning and from quality-bar enforcement.

## Four habits

1. Collect three concrete occurrences before proposing a reusable pattern.
2. Pair every catalog entry with a worked example and explicit exceptions.
3. Review team-level adoption evidence monthly with pattern maintainers.
4. Run a quarterly keep, revise, or retire review for active entries.
