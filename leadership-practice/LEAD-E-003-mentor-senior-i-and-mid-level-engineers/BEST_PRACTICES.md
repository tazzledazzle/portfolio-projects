# LEAD-E-003 — Mentor Senior I and mid-level engineers

## Best practices

- Agree on one role-relevant growth outcome with the mentee and record the evidence that would demonstrate it.
- Use real designs, reviews, incidents, and stakeholder conversations as practice rather than relying on abstract lectures.
- Give timely feedback that names the observed behavior, its impact, and one experiment the mentee can try next.
- Adjust support from pairing to observation to independent ownership as the mentee demonstrates readiness.
- Create safe stretch opportunities, including leading a bounded design or presenting a recommendation, with explicit backup available.
- Review progress with the mentee using their own goals and evidence; do not substitute the mentor's career preferences.
- Keep private notes minimal, respectful, and suitable for sharing with the mentee.

## Four habits

1. Revisit one explicit growth goal in every mentoring cycle.
2. Give one behavior-impact-next-step feedback note while the context is fresh.
3. Move one responsibility from paired work toward independent ownership.
4. Ask the mentee to assess progress before offering the mentor's assessment.
