# LEAD-H-002 — Roadmap and risk management across quarters

## Best practices
- Keep roadmap milestones, dependencies, risks, owners, and evidence in one reviewable cross-quarter register.
- Define leading indicators and kill criteria before optimism or sunk cost makes a roadmap bet difficult to challenge.
- Review dependencies with the teams that control them; do not treat an unconfirmed date as a commitment.
- Escalate risk movement early with evidence, options, and the decision date required to protect downstream milestones.
- Distinguish delivered milestones from scheduled activity so the roadmap reports actual progress.
- Close, transfer, mitigate, or accept each risk explicitly and retain the decision trail for later quarters.

## Four habits
1. Refresh the risk and dependency register before every roadmap review.
2. Test kill criteria against current evidence at each quarterly checkpoint.
3. Escalate a changed risk with options before it becomes a missed milestone.
4. Record delivered milestones and unresolved exposure separately.
