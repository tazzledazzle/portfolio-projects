# LEAD-I-003 — Coach through the design process

## Best practices

- Coach the reasoning process before offering a verdict: ask for the problem, constraints, assumptions, options, and evidence.
- Use questions to expose omitted stakeholders, failure modes, and reversible versus irreversible choices.
- Make the support progression explicit: model a design move, co-author one ADR, review the next, then observe solo reasoning.
- Let the designer state a recommendation and consequences in their own words rather than lending them the coach's authority.
- Separate coaching feedback from the formal review decision so the learner can explore without mistaking guidance for gate approval.
- End each session with the designer's reflection, one next experiment, and evidence that will show stronger independent reasoning.
- Track growth in design judgment without claiming ownership of the design-review process or decision.

## Four habits

1. Ask for constraints and rejected alternatives before sharing a preferred answer.
2. Name the current support level and the next step toward solo ADR ownership.
3. Have the designer restate the recommendation, risks, and unknowns in their own words.
4. Close with one reflection and one observable design-reasoning experiment.
