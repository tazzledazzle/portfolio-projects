# LEAD-I-003: Coach through the design process

**Kind:** implicit | **Type:** evidence kit | **Soft skill:** True

## Evidence from posting
mentoring + design reviews + patterns

## Rationale
Lead shapes how others design, not only verdicts.

## Acceptance / proof
Coaching-through-design worksheet and sample dialogue.

## Owns
The designer's learning process: questions that surface constraints and alternatives, a co-authored-to-solo ADR progression, reflection, and increasing ability to explain design reasoning.

## Does not own
Facilitating or approving the design-review gate (LEAD-E-004), an individual career mentoring playbook (LEAD-E-003), or organization-wide talent multiplication (LEAD-H-003).
