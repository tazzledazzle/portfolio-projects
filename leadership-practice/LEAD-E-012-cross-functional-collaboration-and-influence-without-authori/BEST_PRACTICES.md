# LEAD-E-012 — Cross-functional influence without authority

## Best practices

- Map what each partner values, can offer, is constrained by, and needs protected before making an ask.
- Build reciprocal value through useful context, risk reduction, recognition, access, or delivery help rather than relying on title.
- Frame the shared outcome and propose options with an evidence-backed recommendation.
- Make the exchange explicit enough to avoid hidden obligations, coercion, or promises outside your control.
- Facilitate partner participation in the decision and record dissent, constraints, and commitments.
- Measure influence by voluntary alignment and fulfilled reciprocal commitments, not by consumer-request ranking.

## Four habits

1. Update the currency map before every material cross-functional ask.
2. Name the value offered to each partner alongside the value requested.
3. Present at least two viable options and invite constraints before recommending one.
4. Close the loop by recording voluntary commitments, delivered value, and trust repair needed.
