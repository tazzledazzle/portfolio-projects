# LEAD-E-004 — Own design reviews

## Best practices

- State the decision goal, decision owner, scope, non-goals, and required disposition before inviting reviewers.
- Require a problem frame, at least two credible options, risks, assumptions, and operational consequences before the gate opens.
- Invite the people who build, operate, secure, or consume the design while keeping attendance small enough to decide.
- Timebox context, clarification, option comparison, dissent, and disposition so discussion does not become an unbounded architecture forum.
- Separate blocking concerns from preferences and record dissent without forcing false consensus.
- End with an explicit outcome: approved, approved with actions, revise and return, or no decision because named evidence is missing.
- Publish same-day notes with rationale, owners, due dates, open questions, and a link to the durable decision artifact.

## Four habits

1. Open every review by restating the decision and authority in the room.
2. Check that credible options, risks, and affected operators are represented before debate.
3. Name the disposition and blocking evidence before the meeting ends.
4. Publish rationale, dissent, owners, and due dates the same day.
