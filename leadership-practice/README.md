# Leadership Principles practice

### Contains
- High-leverage technical leadership
- Lead multi-quarter initiatives
- Mentor Senior I and mid-level engineers
- Own design reviews
- Establish engineering patterns
- Raise the quality bar
- Lead post-mortems
- Drive continuous improvement cycles
- Set technical direction
- Make sound architectural trade-offs
- Drive engineering decisions that scale
- Cross-functional collaboration and influence without authority
- Translate technical trade-offs for mixed audiences
- Change management / adoption leadership
- Roadmap and risk management across quarters
