# LEAD-I-004 — Ownership of outcomes, not activity

## Best practices
- State the user or platform outcome, baseline, target, and measurement method before selecting investments.
- Explain the causal hypothesis linking each investment to the goal instead of treating completion as proof of value.
- Review outcome and guardrail deltas on a fixed cadence while keeping task counts as delivery context only.
- Stop, reshape, or replace activity when evidence contradicts the hypothesis, and document the opportunity cost avoided.
- Separate shipped outputs from measured results so an honest narrative can include partial or negative outcomes.
- Publish a concise goal → investments → measured results account with limitations and the next evidence-based decision.

## Four habits
1. Write the outcome baseline and target before approving activity.
2. Review outcome and guardrail deltas instead of task volume.
3. Stop or reshape one investment when evidence breaks its hypothesis.
4. Publish a truthful outcome narrative at each decision boundary.
