# LEAD-E-007 — Lead post-mortems

## Best practices

- Establish psychological safety and state that the review seeks system learning, not a person to blame.
- Reconstruct one incident from timestamped observations, decisions, and impact while separating facts from assumptions.
- Describe contributing conditions across tooling, process, safeguards, and organizational context rather than naming an individual as the cause.
- Convert lessons into specific actions with an owner, due date, expected risk reduction, and observable completion evidence.
- Publish a concise learning record to relevant teams and protect sensitive customer or personnel details.
- Track mitigations until completed, superseded with rationale, or explicitly accepted by the accountable owner.

## Four habits

1. Open every incident review by restating the blameless learning contract.
2. Build the timeline from evidence before discussing causes or countermeasures.
3. Give every accepted action one owner, one due date, and one completion signal.
4. Revisit open actions weekly until each is closed or dispositioned transparently.
