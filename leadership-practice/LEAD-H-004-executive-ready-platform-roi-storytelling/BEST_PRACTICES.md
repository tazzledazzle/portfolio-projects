# LEAD-H-004 — Executive-ready platform ROI storytelling

## Best practices
- State the funding or prioritization decision first, then connect `{{INVESTMENT}}` to a small causal chain of developer and company outcomes.
- Define every measure with owner, source, baseline, time window, method, and confidence so modeled benefits are not presented as observed production results.
- Distinguish observed values, forecasts, assumptions, and excluded benefits in the one-pager.
- Use ranges and sensitivity analysis when attribution or future adoption is uncertain rather than manufacturing a precise ROI.
- Pair financial or capacity framing with reliability, risk, and strategic outcomes when those effects matter to the investment decision.
- Keep supporting calculations traceable in an appendix while making the executive ask readable on one page.
- Keep this artifact about investment and outcomes; do not substitute a dual-altitude technical-decision pair from LEAD-E-013.

## Four habits
1. Reconcile each metric with its owner and source before using it in an executive narrative.
2. Label every value as observed, modeled, forecast, or assumption.
3. Recompute the outcome range when investment, adoption, or attribution assumptions change.
4. End every one-pager with a specific funding or prioritization ask and next review date.
