# LEAD-E-006 — Raise the quality bar

## Best practices

- Define observable criteria for review readiness, test reliability, operability, and release confidence.
- Establish a baseline before changing the bar so improvement claims have a reproducible comparison.
- Apply the bar consistently through review prompts, automation, and exception records.
- Pair stricter expectations with enablement such as examples, coaching, and remediation time.
- Measure outcomes such as escaped defects or flake rate alongside delivery speed to expose harmful local optimization.
- Review the rubric and thresholds when evidence shows they no longer discriminate useful quality.

## Four habits

1. Publish the current rubric and examples where the team performs the work.
2. Capture a dated baseline and measurement method before changing a criterion.
3. Review exceptions and failed checks weekly for enablement gaps.
4. Compare quality and delivery outcomes monthly, then revise one threshold at a time.
