# Proof checklist — LEAD-E-006

- [ ] Attach the dated quality-bar rubric with observable pass criteria and examples.
- [ ] Provide a reproducible baseline for a defect, flake, review, operability, or equivalent quality metric.
- [ ] Provide the after measurement using the same definition and a stated comparison window.
- [ ] Attach evidence of review or automation enforcement plus documented exceptions.
- [ ] Show the enablement supplied so the higher bar was achievable rather than merely announced.
- [ ] Pair the quality result with a delivery guardrail to expose regressions or local optimization.
