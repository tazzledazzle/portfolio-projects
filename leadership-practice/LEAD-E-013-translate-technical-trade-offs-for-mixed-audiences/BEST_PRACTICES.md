# LEAD-E-013 — Translate trade-offs for mixed audiences

## Best practices
- Anchor both artifacts on the identical `{{DECISION}}`, recommendation, and accepted trade-offs so simplification never changes the substance.
- Preserve engineering detail on constraints, alternatives, failure modes, and reversibility in the deep-dive.
- Lead the non-technical one-pager with the decision, user or business impact, risk, timing, and requested response.
- Translate jargon into consequences while retaining links to definitions and the engineering source of truth.
- Test each artifact with a representative reader and record what they understood, questioned, or decided.
- Close the loop with a durable written recap that links the two altitudes and names the decision owner.
- Keep this proof about communication fidelity, not a platform investment, funding, or ROI case.

## Four habits
1. Write the shared decision statement before drafting either audience-specific artifact.
2. Check every simplification against the engineering deep-dive for fidelity.
3. Ask one engineering and one non-technical reader to restate the decision and key risk.
4. Publish a linked decision recap with owner, status, and next step.
